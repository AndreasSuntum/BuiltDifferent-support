Privacy Policy

BUILT DIFFERENT · Last updated: August 25, 2026

BUILT DIFFERENT ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account information: Email address and username when you create an account. Guest mode may store progress locally on your device.
  • App activity: Habits, XP, streaks, ranks, badges, quests, lesson progress, and leaderboard-related data needed to run the game.
  • Health data (optional): If you grant permission, we may read steps, workouts, and active calories from Apple Health or Google Health Connect to power fitness quests. We do not sell health data.
  • Profile: Optional profile photo or emoji avatar you choose.
  • Purchase information: Premium subscription status is processed through Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment card details.

How we use information

  • Provide core app features (habits, focus lock, fitness quests, mindset lessons, ranks, leaderboard)
  • Sync your progress across devices when signed in
  • Process Premium subscriptions and restore purchases
  • Send optional reminders and streak notifications if you enable them
  • Improve stability, fix bugs, and prevent abuse

Third-party services

We may use trusted providers such as:

  • Supabase — account and app data storage
  • RevenueCat / Stripe — subscription and billing management
  • Apple Health / Google Health Connect — optional fitness data sync

These providers process data according to their own privacy policies.

Data retention

We keep your account and progress data while your account is active. You may request account deletion by contacting us. Some data may be retained where required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g. billing records).

Your choices

  • You can use guest mode without creating an account
  • You can deny Health permissions; fitness sync will be limited
  • You can disable notifications in your device settings
  • You can manage or cancel Premium in your App Store / Play Store subscription settings

Children

BUILT DIFFERENT is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
